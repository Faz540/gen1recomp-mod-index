# Kanto Reforged

An expansive overhaul and content expansion for Pokémon Gen 1 and Gen 2 in **Pokémon Gen 1 Recomp**.

## Features

- **Hoenn & Expanded Roster**: Introduces Gen 3 Pokémon, moves, items, and abilities seamlessly into Kanto and Johto.
- **Physical/Special Move Split**: Modern combat dynamics and damage calculations.
- **Restored Kanto Dungeons**: Restores full-sized Generation 1 layouts for Kanto dungeons in Gen 2 (Viridian Forest, Mt. Moon, Rock Tunnel, Seafoam Islands, Pokémon Mansion, and more) with custom encounters and events.
- **DexNav & Overworld Encounters**: Search for Pokémon with hidden abilities, egg moves, and held items right in the field.
- **Battle Mechanics & Abilities**: Full implementation of Gen 3 passive abilities, weather mechanics, and enhanced AI.
- **Postgame Content & Sevii Islands**: New areas to explore, special challenge bosses, and legendary encounters.

## Compatibility & Requirements

- **Supported Games**: Gen 1 (Red/Blue/Yellow) and Gen 2 (Gold/Silver/Crystal).
- **Dependencies**: Requires `pokegear_cards` (for Gen 2 Pokegear card extensions).
- **Permissions**: Requires `engine_internals` and `network` for dynamic asset transforms and online DexNav features.
